Choosing a UX agency is a big decision, and most directories don't make it easier. They sort by service, location or review volume, so a fintech team and a healthcare team end up looking at the same generic list.

**UX Agency Finders starts with your market.** Whether you're building a telemedicine product, a payments platform, a fleet-management tool, a property marketplace, a learning app, a robot interface or an AI product, we show which agencies have done named work in that space and what they actually delivered.

## What we do

- **Curate lists by industry.** We cover eight markets: Healthcare, Fintech, Ecommerce, Logistics, Real Estate, Education, Robotics, and AI & ML.
- **Explain every placement.** Each agency entry names the project, describes the agency's contribution and notes the work stage.
- **Cite our sources.** Every placement links to the agency's own published case study, so you can check it yourself.
- **Label the limits.** Prototypes, portfolio-level entries and service-level evidence are clearly marked.

## Who it's for

Product leaders, founders, design leaders and procurement teams who need a UX partner that already understands their users, workflows and constraints.

## What we don't do

- **We don't sell rankings.** Agencies can't pay to be listed or to move up.
- **We don't sit in the middle.** You contact agencies directly through their official websites. We never collect or forward your details.
- **We don't publish star ratings.** Our positions are editorial judgements with the reasoning shown, not aggregated reviews.
